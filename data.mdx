## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What area does Kviria cover?">
    Kviria covers the **United States** (latitude `24.4` to `49.5`, longitude `-125.0` to `-66.8`). Queries outside coverage return `decision: "none"` (`out_of_coverage`) on `/v1/agent` or `status: "out_of_coverage"` on `/v1/profile` and `/v1/batch`, and count toward your daily credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which tool or endpoint should my agent call?">
    Use `kviria` ([`POST /v1/agent`](/api/agent)) for single-place lookup, nearby/across/next-to reasoning, and multi-turn disambiguation. Use `kviria_profile` ([`POST /v1/profile`](/api/profile)) to inspect street frontage, arterial proximity, building context, walking amenities, and location claims for `1` to `5` places. Use `kviria_batch` ([`POST /v1/batch`](/api/batch)) to ground `2` to `15` stops, sequence a walking itinerary, or find a fair meeting point for `2` to `5` people.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Kviria free?">
    Yes. Kviria is free to use with no credit card or payment option. Every account receives `600` free credits per day (reset at midnight UTC) and `60` requests per minute. See [Rate limits and errors](/api/limits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Kviria store conversations?">
    No. Kviria keeps no conversation text on its servers between turns. The signed `state` string in each `/v1/agent` response carries the turn context, and your agent sends it back on the next call. See the [Privacy Policy](https://kviria.com/privacy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to parse the user's message into fields myself?">
    No. Send `request: "ask"` with the user's message in `words` to `/v1/agent`, and Kviria resolves the area, category, street, address, or follow-up choice automatically. If your agent already extracts structured slots, you can send structured requests (`search`, `find`, `near`, `across`, `next_to`, `corner`, `street_end`, `resolve`, `pick`, `confirm`, `more`) to the same endpoint.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support or report an issue?">
    Email [hello@kviria.com](mailto:hello@kviria.com) for technical support, connector reviews, or data questions. Live uptime and incident history are published at [status.kviria.com](https://status.kviria.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
